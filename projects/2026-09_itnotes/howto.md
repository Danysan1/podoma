[Note](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/IT:Note) sulla mappa da risolvere. Strumenti:
- [ent8r.github.io/NotesReview](https://ent8r.github.io/NotesReview/): mappa con strumenti utili per filtrare e trovare note
- [resultmaps.neis-one.org/osm-notes-country](https://resultmaps.neis-one.org/osm-notes-country?c=Italy): statistiche e ultime note aperte in Italia
- [brzozo.ws/random-note/IT](https://brzozo.ws/random-note/IT): link per aprire una nota random in Italia
- [Notes/Offline analysis](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Notes/Offline_analysis): generare statistiche sulle note e identificare note problematiche
- [notes-heatmap.openstreetmap.fr](https://notes-heatmap.openstreetmap.fr/?pos=42.302225,15.171879,6&flags=ro&precision=4): heatmap delle note
- [observablehq.com/@vgeorge/oldest-osm-notes-by-country](https://observablehq.com/@vgeorge/oldest-osm-notes-by-country?country=Italy): le note aperte più vecchie in Italia
- [better-osm-org](https://community.openstreetmap.org/t/better-osm-org-a-script-that-adds-useful-little-things-to-osm-org/121670/140#p-1138468-notes-filtering-4) permette anche di filtrare le note in un'area in base al contenuto
